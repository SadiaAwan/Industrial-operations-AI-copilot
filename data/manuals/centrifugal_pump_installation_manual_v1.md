# Centrifugal Pump Installation and Commissioning Manual

## 3.2 Foundation and baseplate

The baseplate shall be level, fully supported, and free from distortion. Verify anchor fasteners and grout condition before final alignment.

## 4.1 Pipe connections

Pipework shall not transfer excessive force to the pump casing. Support suction and discharge piping independently and verify pipe strain after connections are complete.

## 5.3 Shaft alignment

Perform initial alignment before connecting piping and final alignment after piping and operating temperature conditions have been considered. Record angular and offset measurements. Recheck alignment after maintenance that disturbs the motor, pump, coupling, or baseplate.

## 6.1 Commissioning checks

Confirm lubrication, guard installation, valve position, pump priming, free shaft rotation, and the approved startup procedure. Never run a pump dry.
