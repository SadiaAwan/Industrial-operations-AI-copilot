# Rotating Equipment Safety Instruction

## 2.1 Prohibited actions

Never remove a guard, touch a coupling, manually rotate a shaft, or perform contact inspection while the equipment can move or become energized.

## 2.2 Required controls

Use the approved lockout/tagout procedure and verify zero energy before physical inspection. Wear the required site personal protective equipment and observe process-area restrictions.

## 2.3 Decision support limitation

Remote sensor review and AI recommendations do not replace site procedures, qualified technical judgement, or authorization to perform work.
