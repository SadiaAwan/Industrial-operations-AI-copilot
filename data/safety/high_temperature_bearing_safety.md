# High Bearing Temperature Safety Instruction

## 3.1 Hazard

An abnormally hot bearing housing can expose personnel to burns and may indicate lubrication failure, bearing damage, friction, or another developing mechanical fault.

## 3.2 Immediate precautions

Keep personnel clear of rotating and hot surfaces. Review the trend and approved operating limits. Follow site escalation and shutdown rules when the safe operating condition is uncertain.

## 3.3 Inspection

Do not touch, open, lubricate, or dismantle the bearing assembly until the pump is isolated, locked out, depressurized where applicable, and verified safe for work.
