# Response to Suspected Pump Cavitation

## 2.1 Recognition

Suspect cavitation when fluctuating vibration occurs with falling suction pressure, unstable discharge pressure, reduced flow, or characteristic crackling noise.

## 2.2 Operational review

Check tank level, inlet valves, suction strainer condition, fluid temperature, and recent process changes. Compare the operating point with the approved pump envelope.

## 2.3 Inspection

Do not open strainers or inspect internal components while energized or pressurized. Apply the approved isolation and lockout/tagout procedure before physical work.

## 2.4 Follow-up

Record the observed values and corrective action. Escalate recurring cavitation for hydraulic-system review rather than repeatedly resetting the pump.
