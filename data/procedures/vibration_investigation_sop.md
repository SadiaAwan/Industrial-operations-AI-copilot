# Pump Vibration Investigation

## 3.1 Confirm the observation

Verify sensor identity, timestamp, unit, and signal quality. Compare the current reading with the machine baseline and recent trend.

## 3.2 Review operating conditions

Record flow, suction pressure, discharge pressure, motor current, and rotational speed. Identify recent startup, process, maintenance, or valve-position changes.

## 3.3 Safe physical checks

Follow the approved lockout/tagout procedure before guard removal or contact inspection. Check fasteners, baseplate, coupling, bearing housing, lubrication, and visible pipe strain.

## 3.4 Escalation

Escalate when vibration continues to rise, temperature is abnormal, evidence conflicts, or the safe operating condition is uncertain. Do not declare a root cause before inspection evidence supports it.
