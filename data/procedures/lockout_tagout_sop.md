# Lockout/Tagout Procedure for Pump Inspection

## 4.1 Preparation

Identify all electrical, mechanical, hydraulic, pneumatic, thermal, and process-energy sources. Notify affected personnel and review the equipment-specific isolation plan.

## 4.2 Isolation before inspection

Stop the pump using the normal procedure. Isolate all energy sources, apply personal locks and tags, release or restrain stored energy, and verify zero-energy state using an approved test method.

Physical inspection, guard removal, coupling access, bearing work, and manual shaft rotation shall not begin until isolation and zero-energy verification are complete.

## 4.3 Return to service

Qualified personnel shall confirm that work is complete, guards are restored, tools are removed, and affected personnel are clear. Locks and tags are removed according to the approved site process before controlled startup.
